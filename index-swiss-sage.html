Our Ethos

Rooted in flavor,
not in animals.

Fushion Café began with one question: why should plant-based food play it safe? We take the char of a Korean grill, the depth of a Vietnamese broth, and the punch of Thai chili — and rebuild every one of them from vegetables, grains, and legumes. No substitutes standing in for the "real thing." This is the real thing.

100% Plant-Based

Every dish, sauce, and broth on the menu — zero animal products, always.

Cooked to Order

Wok-fired street-food style, plated within minutes of your order — never frozen, never steam-tabled.

Regionally Sourced

Seasonal produce from growers within a day's drive, folded into recipes that change with the harvest.

Bold, Not Shy

Chili, citrus, char, and umami built from mushroom, miso, and fermented chili — flavor first.

Our Story

Two high school sweethearts,
one American dream.

Hung Dinh & Van Le
Founders, Fushion Café

Hung Dinh and Van Le fell for each other back in high school, then set out together to build a new life in Houston, Texas. Somewhere along the way, home cooking became a second language — 100% plant-based, out of both love and conviction. After more than fifteen years perfecting recipes at their own table, they opened Fushion Café to bring that same food, and that same welcome, to the rest of the city.

"We wanted more people to see that plant-based food could still taste like home." — Hung & Van, Founders
